The company

Maywood Cycles of Nottingham

We design and build road, city, mountain and children’s bikes in Nottingham, and we are moving into light, connected e-bikes. A new management team, backed by DratLink Capital, took over the business this year to lead that change.

History

From a Nottingham workshop to the e-bike era

  1. 150 years agoMaywood is founded in Nottingham and starts building bicycles by hand.

Strategy

Four moves for the next five years

We keep what makes Maywood strong, a trusted brand and skilled people in Nottingham, and grow where the market is going.

E-bikes

Lead with CYLO

Add CYLO and CYLO-X to the range and grow e-bike sales across Europe, starting with a distribution partner in Germany.

+6%

Grow capacity

Raise output in Nottingham by about 6% through better use of the plant, then add three new sites of up to +15% each.

20%

New channels

Keep our retail partners and add our own website and Maywood stores, each up to 10% of sales.

−0.5%

Lower costs, safely

Cut cost of goods by about 0.5% a year through learning and automation, with safer machines and retrained staff.

Global expansion

Three new sites, one a year from CY+2

True Value Creation

We create £13.2m of true value, but our footprint costs £10.8m

Following the Lindéngruppen model, we add the value we create for society (profit, pay and taxes) and subtract the cost of our environmental and social impacts. The model is set at a £80m revenue scale after expansion.

Value addedImpact costTotal

Carbon is the biggest cost

£5.6m, and 98% of it comes from the materials we buy, such as aluminium and steel.

Materials are the lever

Solar panels on our sites would cut only about 1% of emissions. Recycled aluminium would do far more.

Safety and inclusion count too

Workplace accidents and a 26% female workforce in the UK cost £3.0m together.
